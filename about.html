Our Legacy

From the legendary mines of Ceylon to the finest jewelry collections worldwide.

The Beginning

Founded in the heart of Ratnapura, the "City of Gems", AW_GEMS began with a simple mission: to share the unparalleled beauty of Sri Lankan gemstones with the world. What started as a small family trade has grown into a globally recognized name for quality and authenticity.

Craftsmanship

Every stone in our collection is hand-selected and precision-cut by master artisans. We believe that the cut is what unlocks a gem's soul. Our team combines traditional techniques passed down through generations with modern technology to ensure maximum brilliance.
